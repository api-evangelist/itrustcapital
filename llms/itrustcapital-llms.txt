# iTrustCapital

> iTrustCapital (legal entity ITC2.0, Inc.) is a US fintech software platform for alternative assets, giving self-directed investors 24/7 access to dozens of cryptocurrencies and physical precious metals through tax-advantaged Crypto IRAs, Premium Custody Accounts, and Treasury Accounts for entities. It charges a 1% cryptocurrency transaction fee and no monthly, annual, startup, exit, storage or transfer fees.

> IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS: iTrustCapital publishes **no public developer API**. Its own help center states: "At this time iTrustCapital does not offer any API integration to any third-party applications, including self-trading bots and coin trackers." There is no OpenAPI, no GraphQL endpoint, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no SDK, no CLI and no webhook surface. Do not attempt to construct or infer one. Account access is through the web application and the iOS/Android mobile apps only.

## Products

- [Crypto IRA](https://www.itrustcapital.com/ira): Self-directed tax-advantaged individual retirement account for cryptocurrencies and physical precious metals.

- [Premium Custody Account](https://www.itrustcapital.com/premium-custody-account): Non-retirement everyday investing account.
- [Treasury Account](https://www.itrustcapital.com/treasury): For businesses, trusts, non-profits and other entities.
- [Precious Metals](https://www.itrustcapital.com/precious-metals): Physical gold, silver and platinum.
